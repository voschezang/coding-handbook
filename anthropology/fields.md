# Fields

The **art** appears because each field is rarely a bounded specialization. Specialists must balance people, organizations and the environment. See also [accountabilities](../management/accountability-triangle.md).


| Field                  | Purpose                                 | Challenge                                                    |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Art of **governance**  | Accountability and organization         | Empowerment, control, institutions                           |
| Art of **management**  | Facilitate delivery of value            | Focus (delegation), adaption, collaboration                  |
| Art of **leadership**  | Alignment and direction                 | Authority and influence, progress                            |
| Art of **business**    | Make money now as well as in the future | Balance needs of customers, competiton, supplyers, governments |
| Art of **engineering** | Solve problems reliably                 | Produce results with minimal effort and minmal complexity    |
| Art of **science**     | Find objective truth, understanding     | Every discovery creates more questions                       |
| Art of **philosophy**  | Questioning and language                | Deconstruct everything without losing meaning                |
| **Art**                | *L'art pour l'art*                      | Expression, imitation                                        |
