# Fields

The **art** appears because each field is rarely a bounded specialization. Specialists must balance people, organizations and the environment. See also [accountabilities](../management/accountability-triangle.md).


| Field                                                        | Purpose                                 | Challenge                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Art of **governance**                                        | Accountability and organization         | Empowerment, control, institutions                           |
| Art of [**management**](../management/management-principles.md) | Facilitate delivery of value            | Focus (delegation), adaption, collaboration                  |
| Art of **leadership**                                        | Alignment and direction                 | Authority and influence, progress                            |
| Art of **business**                                          | Make money now as well as in the future | Balance needs of customers, competiton, supplyers, governments |
| Art of [**economics**](economics.md)                         | Define and explain the economy          | Politics. Decouple monetary policy from social policy        |
| Art of [**engineering**](../software-industry/software-engineering.md) | Solve problems reliably                 | Produce results with minimal effort and minmal complexity    |
| Art of **science**                                           | Find objective truth, understanding     | Every discovery creates more questions                       |
| Art of **philosophy**                                        | Questioning and language                | Deconstruct everything without losing meaning                |
| **Art**                                                      | *L'art pour l'art*                      | Expression, imitation                                        |

Science tends to be **reductionist**. Break down everything into components and study them individually. E.g. decouple biology, psychology and medicine. Decouple economics from ethics. The humanities have become more holistic. E.g. observing people in their own environment. By analogy, consider the wave-particle [duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave%E2%80%93particle_duality). Particles adhere to strict laws, while waves are probabilistic and interconnected.
