## Triangle: Threat/Drive/Calm Systems

The being-becoming model can be extended by a third component: threat responses. See [nervous-system-triangle](nervous-system-triangle.md).

<img src="../img/triangle-threat-drive-calm.png" alt="triangle-threat-drive-calm" style="width:39em;" />

| Threat System          | Drive System (Becoming)    | Calm System (Being) |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| React to threats       | Progress towards rewards   | Relax               |
| Fight/flight responses | Dopamine seeking behaviour | Peace, rest         |

### Quadrants. Reality and Imagination

The calm system can be divided in grounded, present calmth and states of dreaming and imagination. This yields two independent dimensions. See [being-becoming](being-becoming.md).

- Being and becoming. Being refers to the present and becoming to the non-present.
- Calm (rested or dreaming) and restless (threats and reward seeking)
